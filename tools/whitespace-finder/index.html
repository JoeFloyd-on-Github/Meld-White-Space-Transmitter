TV White Space Finder

A first-order look at which TV channels may be open for white-space use at a location, computed from the latest FCC protected-entity data mirrored in ./data/ (TV stations, 600 MHz licensees, wireless mics, BAS links, MVPD/TV receive sites). Part of the Meld White Space Transmitter project.

Informational only — not an FCC white-space determination. A legal transmission still requires a certified white-space database and registration, and this model is a simplified distance-based approximation of the FCC §15.712 protection rules, not the real contour math. For the Meld MT300 specifically, its FCC grant (OKVMT300) authorizes only channels 21–34 and 39–51.

Method & data

For each channel, every TV station on that channel (US/CA/MX, all services) gets an estimated protected-contour radius from its ERP, HAAT and band; the channel is OCCUPIED at your point if you fall within that radius plus the co-channel margin. A first-adjacent (±1) station inside its contour flags ADJACENT. Wireless-mic, BAS, MVPD-receive, TV-receive and land-mobile registrations add point keep-outs on their channel(s). Channel 37 is reserved; channels 38–51 are the repacked 600 MHz band (licensed mobile) and are flagged regardless of TV occupancy. The contour estimate is empirical, not the FCC F(50,90) curves — treat verdicts as guidance, and see README.md. Data snapshot in ./data/, source csv.wavedb.com.
